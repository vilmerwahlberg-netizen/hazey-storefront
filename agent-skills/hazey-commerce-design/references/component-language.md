# Current component language

This is the durable bridge between accepted pilots and new pages. It records reusable intent, not every current pixel. `decisions.md` and accepted reference files outrank this summary when they conflict.

## Global shell

- The desktop header is one compact centered glass bubble. It adapts foreground contrast to the scene below and uses the established semi-sticky scroll behavior. Page-specific work must not recolor, reshape, duplicate, or replace it.
- Mobile keeps its accepted compact header composition. Do not infer mobile from desktop glass geometry.
- Footer structure, links, payment trust, and platform integration remain global. Page work may correct a proven seam or overflow but must not redesign the footer incidentally.
- Persistent UI uses an explicit edge priority. On mobile, purchase conversion normally outranks chapter shortcuts; on desktop, header and filter dock must coexist without overlap.

## Canvas and layout

- The base is a warm, nuanced sand field rather than pure white or one flat beige slab. Tone shifts, light, fine texture, and restrained botanical shadows may create chapters without turning every chapter into a container.
- Use the semantic Theme 8 palette from `theme8/css/00-foundation.css`: olive for brand authority and secondary action, controlled burnt orange for conversion emphasis, ink for readable depth, warm beige/neutral mixes for canvas and surfaces, and status colors only for their real meaning. Do not copy historical hex values into a component or introduce a separate page palette.
- Color distribution is part of the design: warm light fields should dominate ordinary browsing, green should anchor hierarchy, and orange should remain scarce enough to identify the primary commercial action. The West Coast feeling comes from light, material, photography, tone variation, and composition—not from pushing saturation.
- Reading content, commerce grids, breadcrumbs, and utility rows share deliberate alignment and a large-screen width cap. Full-bleed heroes, imagery, and decoration are explicit exceptions.
- Desktop is composed at 1280, 1440, 1512, and 1920 rather than scaled continuously. Mobile is composed at 320, 393, and 430; tablet and 200% zoom must remain functional.
- Avoid visible one-pixel tone seams between adjacent warm surfaces and blank strips below the footer.

## Product cards

- Preserve stable image-stage proportions, product identity, price, real rating when available, stock, relevant tags, and a clear purchase or selection action.
- Purchase actions are compact, rectangular or softly rounded, text-labelled, and may use restrained gradient/light/shadow for tactile character. A lone circular plus action is rejected.
- Card height must be driven by a deliberate content contract, not one accidental product name. Test long Swedish names, missing rating, sale, sold-out, and multiple badges.
- Hover may add subtle depth on fine pointers; touch receives immediate press feedback without a stuck hover state.

## Category and series discovery

- `Alla Vapes` is the unified shopping surface for vapes, carts, and refills. Its in-flow filters remain the primary control; the desktop dock is a compact continuation after scrolling.
- Series hubs may use brand storytelling and format shelves while retaining the same header, type hierarchy, product-card language, width system, and commerce clarity as the rest of the store.
- Series × format pages use a reusable listing template. Hero/copy, available filters, result count, empty states, and optional sibling-series exploration are data-driven. Authored hub modules are not duplicated blindly onto every listing.
- Future assortment such as D10 or additional Magic Sauce strains must appear through real data and defined optional modules, not fabricated placeholder products.

## Product detail pages

- The Faraoh pilot is the current structural reference: balanced gallery and purchase station, compact centered strain carousel, transparent package economics, mixed-strain package support when real bundle products exist, and a mobile sticky purchase action.
- The gallery height is independent of package-builder height. Selecting a 3- or 5-pack must not resize the primary product image.
- Product overview may blend botanical imagery and cast shadow into the warm canvas. Avoid a pasted landscape rectangle or a high-contrast campaign block beneath the gallery.
- Cross-sell and related-product cards appear before deep reviews when that supports discovery, while a clear review link near the rating jumps to genuine reviews.
- Long product information uses semantic accordions/progressive disclosure while keeping crawlable initial-DOM content and avoiding duplicate facts across summary, overview, and accordions.

## Status boundaries

- Accepted pilots are references, not universal hardcoded content.
- A component may be visually accepted while its data integration remains provisional. State that distinction explicitly.
- The reusable series × format system and Magic Sauce extensions are still being refined; do not treat an unreviewed agent screenshot as a new golden baseline.
