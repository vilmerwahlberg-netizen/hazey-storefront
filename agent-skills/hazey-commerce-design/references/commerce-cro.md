# Commerce, CRO, and trust

## Primary visitor path

The homepage must quickly answer:
1. What does Hazey sell and why is it credible?
2. How do I find the right format, cannabinoid, series, or product?
3. What is available now?
4. Why is ordering safe, legal, transparent, and dependable?
5. What should I do next?

Visual novelty may strengthen these answers but must not delay them.

## Broad assortment

Hazey's breadth is a competitive advantage. Do not copy a narrow single-product funnel from a competitor. Borrow applicable patterns, then adapt them to discovery across independent axes: format, product type, series, and cannabinoid.

Use hierarchy rather than showing everything at once. Strong entry routes, search, curated series, bestsellers, and progressive disclosure can coexist without making the page a catalog wall.

## Trust hierarchy

Distribute evidence by decision moment:
- Above the fold: shortest credible signals.
- Product cards: availability, real rating, relevant delivery expectation, verified analysis status where available.
- Dedicated trust/process section: Swedish fulfilment, no cross-border shipping, 1–2 day delivery where truthful, delivery guarantee, analysis and content transparency.
- Reviews: real Trustpilot or verified product-review data with clear source; never fabricate badges or quotes.
- Footer/policies: complete conditions and durable destinations.

Avoid repeating the same four claims word-for-word. Increase detail as the shopper moves deeper.

## Conversion guardrails

- A primary CTA must be visually obvious in every selling moment.
- Product cards retain enough information to choose: image, identity, rating when real, price, stock, relevant promotion, CTA, and delivery cue.
- Do not bury products beneath long brand essays, decorative pinned scroll sequences, or excessive pre-purchase education.
- Do not add countdowns, fake scarcity, forced autoplay, modal spam, or deceptive urgency.
- Carousels require visible manual control, correct looping, keyboard and touch support, and meaningful static fallback.
- Mobile touch targets and vertical scrolling take priority over decorative gestures.
- Any new conversion hypothesis should be measurable and framed as a test, not asserted as truth.

## Competitor use

Study competitors for information architecture, trust placement, interaction patterns, and section sequencing. Do not copy proprietary code, copywriting, branding, or assets. For Lyfted specifically, adapt useful product-focus, process, trust, and editorial patterns to Hazey's broader assortment and own identity.
