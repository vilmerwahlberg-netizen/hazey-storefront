# Source notes

This original Hazey-specific skill was informed by, but does not bundle or execute, two third-party projects:

- Impeccable by Paul Bakaus, Apache-2.0: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
- Taste Skill by Leonxlnx, MIT: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill

Useful adopted ideas include separating durable product truth from visual direction, choosing design mode before editing, critique based on rendered evidence, anti-template discipline, bounded verification, purposeful motion, and image-first concept exploration.

No third-party binary, hook, installer, dependency, or substantial verbatim skill payload is included. Consult upstream projects for their full commands, detectors, browser tooling, and licenses.
