# Source notes

This Hazey-specific skill was informed by, but does not bundle or execute, these third-party projects:

- Impeccable by Paul Bakaus, Apache-2.0: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
- Taste Skill by Leonxlnx, MIT: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill
- Taste design-DNA extractor by Senlin, MIT: https://github.com/senlindesign/taste-skill
- Design-engineering and animation skills by Emil Kowalski, MIT: https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills

Useful adopted ideas include separating durable product truth from visual direction, choosing design mode before editing, critique based on rendered evidence, anti-template discipline, bounded verification, extracting trade-offs rather than copying tokens, motion opportunity gates, frequency-aware animation, physical origins, interruptibility, and image-first concept exploration.

No third-party binary, hook, installer, dependency, or substantial verbatim skill payload is included. Consult upstream projects for their full commands, detectors, browser tooling, and licenses.
