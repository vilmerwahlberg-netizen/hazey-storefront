# Art direction

## Brand world

Build from a specific intersection rather than a generic premium mood:

- California golden-hour warmth and coastal landscape.
- Independent hip-hop/record-label confidence: editorial crops, sleeve-like compositions, rhythm, and cultural specificity without graffiti clichés.
- Botanical and material cues: paper, smoked glass, sun-faded pigment, canvas, wood, metal, product packaging, macro plant texture.
- Swedish trust and restraint: readable facts, direct language, calm controls, no hype machinery.

Working name: **California Editorial Apothecary / West Coast Counterculture Commerce**. This is a prompt for exploration, not a locked style name.

## Anti-reference: the rejected middle state

Do not preserve these merely because they exist today:
- Warm cream background + heritage serif + terracotta used everywhere as shorthand for premium.
- A vertical sequence of similar rounded white/cream cards.
- Every section receiving equal visual weight.
- Shadows, border-radius, and fade-up as the main evidence of design.
- Tiny typography and compressed cards copied from a mobile facit onto desktop.
- Generic Shopify, wellness-DTC, SaaS, or dispensary-template composition.

Hazey may retain cream, olive, serif, and orange as brand materials, but redistribute them with a distinct thesis. A color may be correct while its proportion and repetition are wrong.

## Concept requirements

Each proposed direction must answer:
1. What is the single recognizable Hazey gesture?
2. What leads the first viewport?
3. Where does the page change pace?
4. Which sections are open editorial fields, commerce grids, or enclosed surfaces?
5. How does desktop become a deliberate composition rather than enlarged mobile?
6. What builds friendliness and trust without becoming generic?
7. What one motion idea belongs specifically to this world?

Concepts must differ structurally, not just in colors. Use the same real content to make comparison fair.

## Recommended exploration range

- Layout variance: 7/10. Asymmetric and memorable, never chaotic.
- Motion intensity: 5/10. One focal moment plus restrained responsive feedback.
- Information density: 5/10. Broad assortment remains discoverable; long explanations use progressive disclosure.

## Typography

Do not automatically keep or remove Iowan Old Style. Test it against at least one characterful non-generic sans/display alternative using real Swedish copy. Typography must support product authority and culture, not imitate an apothecary template. Use at most two type families and define clear roles.

## Imagery

Treat photography and product packaging as primary design material. Prefer art-directed real imagery. Preserve product identity and crop integrity. Do not use random stock photos, unrelated AI products, cannabis-leaf wallpaper, or one generic image per card merely to fill space.
